I'm Ben Hollar, a full-stack software developer who calls Cincinnati, Ohio home.

## Professional Work

I've been working as a software developer for over 9 years, specializing in creating geospatial data visualization and
analysis tools. The software I've helped build empowers data analysts to find hidden relationships between different
datasets, and to quickly refine algorithm-generated data into reliable reports.

I began my career while studying at the University of Cincinnati, where I graduated with a Bachelor's of Science in
Computer Science in the spring of 2021. While studying, I worked as a co-op at
[Etegent Technologies Ltd.](https://etegent.com), a research and development company that specializes in creating
machine learning and data analytics tools for the Department of Defense.

Since my graduation, I've worked full-time at Etegent where I've worked on a variety of projects. Among many other
things, I've built:
* A full-stack application to ingest large geospatial datasets, compute
  [intensity maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_map) of them, and visualize them using 
  [`cesium.js`](https://cesium.com/).
* A [PyQt6](https://www.riverbankcomputing.com/software/pyqt/) user interface to allow analysts to view and refine
  algorithmically-generated track data
* A full-stack web app to configure an aerodynamics modelling tool and visualize its outputs as an animated 3D
  simulation using [`three.js`](https://threejs.org/)

## Personal Life

Even at home, I find myself frequently sitting in front of a computer, watching copious amounts of YouTube videos,
playing single-player video games, crafting my homebrew
[Ghosts of Saltmarsh](https://dnd.wizards.com/products/ghosts-saltmarsh) D&D 5e campaign, or idly dreaming of personal
software development projects.

When I'm not in front of a computer, you'll likely find me spending time with my wife, Roxanne, and our black
labrador retriever mix, Rudi. We love watching movies, searching for waterfalls on scenic hikes, and playing board games
like [Wingspan](https://stonemaiergames.com/games/wingspan/) or
[Ravine](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/237728/ravine), to name a few.

I also enjoy taking [photos](/photos/) -- especially of wildlife. It's a great excuse to get outside and
appreciate the environment around us. Beyond that, I've also been known to [sing](https://youtu.be/oel1JmjWdgs?t=2083),
play the trombone, and whistle idly almost all day long.
